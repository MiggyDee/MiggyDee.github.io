Privacy Policy

Secretary Agent (Ate Sonnet) · Last updated November 12, 2026

Secretary Agent is a private, non-commercial personal assistant on Telegram, run by one person (the "operator") for themselves and a few approved friends. This policy explains what it does with your data, including data from Google Calendar.

What it collects

  • Your messages to the Telegram bot, including screenshots, PDFs and voice notes, so it can understand what to remind you about.
  • What it saves for you: reminders, due dates, appointments, and anything else you ask it to keep (such as lists, notes or spending entries).
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it: the events it creates for you.

Google Calendar access

  • Secretary Agent requests the calendar.app.created permission. This lets it create a separate calendar and manage events only on calendars it created. It cannot read, change or delete your other calendars or events.
  • It uses this access only to add, update and remove the reminders and appointments you asked for, and to check that they were saved correctly.
  • Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • You can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the calendar it created; your other calendars are not affected.

How your data is processed

  • Everything Secretary Agent saves is stored on the operator's own computer, not on a public server.
  • To understand your messages, their content is sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) under that provider's terms. Messages are sent to Telegram to deliver replies and reminders.
  • Passwords, PINs, one-time codes and account, card or ID numbers are never saved.
  • Your data is never shown to other users.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until you delete it or ask the operator to remove it. You can ask Secretary Agent in Telegram to forget things, and you can stop using it at any time. Backups, including copies of chats with the bot, are kept on the operator's own computer and are never published; ask the operator if you want yours removed.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, message the operator through the Secretary Agent Telegram bot.
