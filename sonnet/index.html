Secretary Agent (Ate Julia)

Secretary Agent, called Ate Julia, is a personal secretary that a small group of people chat with on Telegram. You send her a screenshot of a bill, a PDF statement, a chat where you made plans, or just type a message, and she turns it into reminders so nothing gets forgotten.

What she does

  • Reads bills, due dates and plans from messages, screenshots and PDFs.
  • Sends reminders on Telegram before things are due.
  • For people who connect Google Calendar, adds those reminders and appointments as events on a separate calendar she creates for them.
  • Keeps a private money notebook, lists and notes for each person.

Google Calendar

Secretary Agent asks for permission to manage only the calendars it creates. It cannot see or change your other calendars. It uses that access to add, update and remove the events you asked for, and nothing else. See the privacy policy for details.

Who can use it

Secretary Agent is a private, non-commercial assistant run by one person for themselves and a few friends. It is not offered to the public, and new people can only use it after the operator approves them.
